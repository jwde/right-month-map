Scores combine daytime temperature, humidity, rain and sunshine. They are long-term averages for 1961–1990 on a grid of about 35 km, so recent years run a little warmer, and small islands and steep mountains are approximate. Crowds, prices, storm seasons and smoke are not included. Climate data: CRU CL v2.0 (New et al., 2002). Place names: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0. Outlines and islands: Natural Earth. Borders are approximate and do not imply any position on disputed territory.
